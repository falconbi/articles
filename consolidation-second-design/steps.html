01

The idea

In one line. Add everything together. Remove what is internal to the group. Recognise who owns what. Produce the group statements.

Each adjustment is its own layer (a Stage). The companies' own books are never changed.

04

The fifteen steps

1. Identify the group structure ✅

Holdings are entered once: owner, company, share %, vote %. Rules work out, for each group, control %, interest % and classification.

Votes held by a controlled company count in full. Subsidiary above 50% control, associate from 20%, otherwise investment. Overrides are possible. A group (Root) exists for each holding company whose accounts are needed.

2. Get each entity's trial balance ✅ (upload ❌)

Loaded into the Data cube at Root = Entity Level, Stage = Reported, by account and month. Amounts with another group company carry a Partner tag.

3. Standardise accounting policies ✅

Company journals (account, debit, credit, partner, narrative) are posted to Stage = Company Adj. Reported + Company Adj + Translation = Adjusted Entity: the figures the group uses.

4. Convert currencies ✅

ItemRate
Assets, liabilitiesclosing
Equity at acquisitionacquisition date
Profit since acquisitioneach month's average, locked
Differencetranslation reserve (owners / NCI)

Written to Stage = Translation. Goodwill of a foreign subsidiary stays in its own currency and is retranslated at closing. A sub-group can translate step by step through its own currency.

5. Aggregate the financial statements ✅

Each member's adjusted books are copied into the group root. The Entity dimension keeps each company's part; All Entities is the group total. Only the parent and subsidiaries are copied.

6. Eliminate the parent's investment ✅

Stage = Investment Elimination. The investment comes off the owner; the subsidiary's equity at acquisition comes off the subsidiary. If an intermediate the group only partly owns paid for it, that intermediate's NCI carries its share of the cost.

7. Goodwill / bargain purchase ✅

Goodwill = cost (+ NCI at fair value, if that method) − net assets at the date the group gained control. Negative goodwill is a gain. One business combination gives one goodwill. Fair value adjustments are added at acquisition and unwind over time (Stage = FVA Realisation). Impairments use Stage = Impairment.

8. Recognise NCI ✅

NCI at acquisition: fair value, or share of net assets. Chosen per acquisition.

9. Eliminate intercompany balances ✅ (matching screen ❌)

Stage = Intragroup. Every amount whose partner is in the same group is removed on both sides. A mismatch goes to an Intragroup Difference account, with a warning.

Only pairs inside the group being consolidated. A sub-group's sales to its parent stay as sales in the sub-group's own accounts.

10. Eliminate intercompany transactions ✅

As step 9, for P&L lines: sales and purchases, fees, interest, dividends.

11. Eliminate unrealised profit ✅

Entered as an event: seller, buyer, amount. The seller's profit is reduced, the buyer's stock (or land) is reduced, and the seller's NCI carries its share. Next month it reverses — the profit is realised — unless entered again.

12. NCI share of movements ✅

Stage = NCI Post-Acq. NCI % × the change in the subsidiary's equity since acquisition, taken from each equity component so group reserves split correctly.

Balance sheet and P&L, by stage, from the Data cube at Stage = Consolidated. Not yet: statement of changes in equity, notes. Cash flow is out of scope.

05

Year-end close

Run automatically in the first month of a year. Next year's opening balance = this year's closing, for every group and every layer. P&L and dividends go into retained earnings.

06

Checks

Three layers.

1. Every run ✅

CheckIf it fails

Every calculation writes a line to the Run Log: what was done, with the figures used — goodwill, NCI, rates, eliminations. A failed run says why, in plain words.

2. Every change ✅

The 26 worked examples stay in the model as their own versions. Each has a known answer from the source.

  • Automated checks — 133 stored queries, each with an expected answer. They run every time the model is changed. One failure blocks the change.
  • Check views — one per example: the book's figure, the model's figure, the difference.

3. Live data ❌ planned

  • Health checks — rules that must hold on any data: balance, intercompany difference zero, opening = last year's closing, holdings ≤ 100%, every subsidiary has an acquisition, no errors in the last run.
  • Reconciliations — NCI and retained earnings: opening + movements = closing.
  • Review and sign-off — per group, per period.
